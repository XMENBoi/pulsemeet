Tips

Improve OCR accuracy

  • Use scans that are upright, well-lit, and high contrast.
  • Crop out large backgrounds or shadows before upload when possible.
  • For multipage PDFs, wait for page previews to finish before starting OCR.

Privacy

Client-side processing first

This interface is designed to keep documents in the browser during extraction. OCR libraries are loaded from a CDN at runtime.
